# GetYr Studio: quality bar (applies to everything we make)

**Every shot must look like real footage, so real that viewers are shocked it was made in Blender.**
If a shot looks "3D", "game-like", "toy-like" or "built", it is not finished.

## Rules
1. **Real-world reference first.** Every scene copies real photos/footage: real scale (metres), real camera lenses
   (24-85 mm), real heights, real speeds.
2. **Real materials only.** Photo-scanned textures (Poly Haven / CC0), real HDRI skies, measured values for
   roughness and reflections. No flat colours, no procedural "plastic".
3. **Real light.** One clear sun + real sky, physically based (Cycles), enough samples to be noise-free,
   aerial haze and depth for distance.
4. **Imperfection = realism.** Dirt, wear, uneven edges, dust, wet patches, small vibrations, camera shake
   from real rigs, lens effects (motion blur, depth of field, slight bloom, film grain).
5. **Camera like a real crew.** Drone, car rig, handheld, crane: only moves a real camera could make.
6. **No visible tricks.** No repeating textures, no floating objects, no clipping, no hard CG edges,
   no perfectly clean surfaces.
7. **Real sound.** Recorded-sounding ambience and effects matching every shot.
8. **Check before publishing.** Every shot is compared against real reference footage; anything that
   "feels CG" is redone, however long it takes (2-3 days per video is fine).
