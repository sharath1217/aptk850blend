# GetYr Studio: content plan

**What we make:** short, clear, beautiful **animated explainers** (motion graphics, made from code).
Every video: a 3-5 min YouTube film (16:9) + a 45-60 s Short/Reel (9:16). Cost per video: $0.

## Our formula (every video)
1. **Hook in 5 seconds:** a surprising fact or question ("Your phone has 19 billion tiny switches").
2. **One clear idea**, explained in steps, with a real-life analogy (water tap → transistor).
3. **Show, don't tell:** every sentence has a moving visual (map, diagram, counter, chart, timeline).
4. **Natural, fast narration**, spoken like a friend, not a textbook. Word-by-word captions.
5. **Payoff + callback** at the end, then "Follow GetYr Studio".
6. **Accuracy first:** facts double-checked; uncertain numbers say "about" or "estimated".

## Formats
- **Deep explainer** (3-5 min): "How a transistor works", "The last 2 hours 40 minutes of Titanic"
- **Short / Reel** (30-60 s): **written and voiced separately** (never cropped or cut from the film). Its own punchy script,
  hook in the first second, vertical-first layout, faster pace, word-by-word captions, loop-friendly ending
- **Monthly special** (6-8 min): bigger story with more scenes

## Schedule (automatic)
- 1 deep explainer per series per week + 3-5 Shorts per week (cut from the films)
- Published to Releases / YouTube automatically once the upload is connected

## Pipeline
topic queue → AI script (hook, analogy, steps) → editor + fact check → scene plan (scene library) →
voice (Edge TTS now; Kokoro / Chatterbox later) → Remotion render on GitHub → music + captions → film + Short → upload
